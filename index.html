How to Play

⚜️

Welcome to the Realm

A quick guide to get you started on your journey.

1

Choose Your Faction

Pick the team that matches your real RCM function: Medical Coding (🦉), Charge Capture (🦅), Collections (🦁), Cash Application (🐉), or Patient Financial Services (🧸❤️). Each faction has unique stat bonuses that give you advantages in specific activities.

2

Explore the World Map

The map has locations you can visit (Training Mesa, Audit Outpost, Deep Dive Canyon, etc.). Click a location to access mini-games like Trivia, Word Scramble, Rapid Fire, and Match Pairs. You can also click monsters on the map to battle them.

3

Earn XP Through Real Work

The most valuable XP comes from Tasks — real-world work like audits, coaching sessions, deep dives, and WBR submissions. Complete the work, then submit proof (email subject or document link). Your Guild Founder reviews and approves before points are awarded.

4

Complete Quests

Check the Quest Board (📜) for daily, weekly, epic, and legendary quests. Hover over any quest to see detailed instructions on how to complete it. Legendary quests like the Close-the-Loop Deep Dive award the most XP.

5

Level Up Your Skills

The Skills tab (📖) shows your function-specific training track from the SME Development Program 2.0. Complete e-learning modules, attend ILT sessions, and finish assignments to earn XP and build expertise in your area.

6

Collaborate Across Teams

Go to Factions (⚔️) to log cross-team collaborations. Every time you work with someone from another faction (joint audit, shared deep dive, calibration session), log it for +30 XP. Both teams get bonus points.

7

Fight the Boss

Each quarter has a Boss tied to a real KPI challenge. Click "Join Boss Raid" to answer trivia questions that deal damage. The whole guild works together to defeat the boss and unlock rewards like badges and certification eligibility.

8

Climb the Leaderboard

Your XP contributes to both your individual rank and your team's total. Check Ranks (🏆) to see where you stand. Check Teams (🏴) to see which faction is winning the quarterly competition. Top performers earn real-world recognition.

📜 Key Rules

• No proof, no points. Tasks require tangible evidence (email, link, screenshot).

• Guild Founder reviews all task submissions. Points are awarded after approval.

• Mini-games award XP instantly. Trivia, scrambles, and raids don't need approval.

• Stat bonuses matter. Your faction gives you real advantages in specific activities.

• Streaks reward consistency. Log in daily to build your streak multiplier.

• Collaboration is rewarded. Cross-team work earns bonus points for both factions.

• This tracks real work. The game doesn't add new work — it rewards what you already do.
